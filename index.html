ShiftMind: Shared Workforce Memory and Agentic Analysis on Smart Glasses

Jose Manuel Davila†  ·  Eduardus Tjitrahardja†  ·  Yuntao Wang
Tsinghua University  ·  † equal contribution
SHARED MEMORY QUESTION

When a returning customer is served by a different employee, possibly at a different site, how should the record of prior encounters reach that employee, without a query, and without asking the customer to repeat context?

Download print PDF (A4) Code on GitHub

We argue that consumer smart glasses, already issued to frontline staff for task support, are a candidate platform for organizational memory.
ShiftMind contributes the framing, an end-to-end open-source prototype with a strict I/O-compute split, and a shared-memory framework: identity-anchored, cumulative per customer, and queryable by a tool-using agent.
Scope: research prototype, evaluated at small scale; a user study and in-store evaluation are future work.
